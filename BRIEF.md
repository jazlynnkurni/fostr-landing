# Fostr Landing: Brief

## What this is
The landing demo website for raising money for Fostr (Social Bricks). It must be
impressive, deeply interactive, and cohesive with the message. This page IS the pitch.

## Brand
- Font: Plus Jakarta Sans
- Primary color: #5DA1A1 (teal)
- Logo: "Fostr" wordmark, dark text, with a small teal two-leaf sprout above the "o"
- Tone: serious empathy. Unglamorous, load-bearing, honest. Never bleak, never cutesy.

## The vision (founder's words kept intact)
Roots growing downward. Why roots: roots do the opposite work; they're what's underneath,
unglamorous, load-bearing, invisible when they're doing their job.

The page scrolls DOWN into the earth. Each panel's tooltip is a question that the next
panel answers, a continuous conversational thread from problem to founder story to CTA.

## The 8 panels (founder copy, verbatim)

1. HERO. Badge: "early access". Above Ground (Problem)
   "Most of a caseworker's day never reaches a kid."
   Tooltip: where does it go?

2. (Problem)
   "It goes into forms. Intake, case notes, court prep, the state's system — the same
   facts, typed over and over."
   Tooltip: whose fault is that?

3. (Problem)
   "Not the worker's. No one chose this career for their love of paperwork."
   Tooltip: so what do you do?

4. (Solution from here forward)
   "Fostr takes it down once, during the visit, and sends it everywhere it's owed."
   Tooltip: and it's accurate?

5. "Court filings can't be mostly right. Every line Fostr produces traces back to
   something in the record. No guessing."
   Tooltip: who is this for?

6. "We're starting with child welfare, alongside the people already doing the work.
   Group homes, elder care, anywhere reporting is eating the job — that comes next."
   Tooltip: why us / me / you? (flexible)

7. Deepest, darkest, most intimate panel:
   "I was in the Massachusetts foster care system. The people responsible for me cared.
   They also had a stack of forms taller than I was. That's why Fostr exists."
   — Jaden, founder

8. CTA: "Book 20 minutes with Jaden" / "Email Jaden"

## Interaction requirements
- Scroll-driven: effects appear and evolve as the user scrolls (scrollytelling).
- 3D welcome: Three.js on the web, shaders allowed.
- Framer Motion for interactive card components.
- Must feel top-notch to investors: fast load, works on a phone handed across a table,
  honors prefers-reduced-motion.
